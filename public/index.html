Hi, I'm Miriam

I'm a Data Science student at Boston University with a minor in Math and German. I'm interested in the power of data to tell impactful stories. My primary interests are civic tech, data visualization and anything to do with transportation.

As a research assistant in the Department of Political Science, I'm working to build a workable dataset from handwritten historical records. This project has taught me a lot about the complications that come up when working with historical data systems and how to separate important patterns from noise.

I'm also involved as website staff with both BU's chapter of Engineers Without Borders and the Pinky Toe paper. I find working in the intersection between the creative and technical very rewarding. These positions have also taught me how to work within large teams and handle communication when decisions need to be approved on multiple levels.
